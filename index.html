Features

Focused tools built for studying, with a clean developer-grade UI.

PDF / Textbook Summarizer

Upload PDFs or textbook text and get concise summaries, chapter outlines, and flashcard-ready notes.

Homework Helper (text + images)

Explain problems step-by-step and analyze diagrams or screenshots — all processed locally.

Exam Generator (difficulty-based)

Create quizzes and full exams tailored by difficulty, topic coverage, and question type.

Study Planner (Pomodoro-style)

Generate study plans with Pomodoro sessions, breaks, and subject prioritization.

Read-For-You (Text-to-Speech)

Listen to chapters or notes with adjustable speed and voice options — processed locally.

System requirements

Choose the build that matches your machine. AI runs locally — performance scales with RAM.

Lite

For lightweight usage
RAM: 4 GB

Best for quick summaries and small PDFs.

Standard

Balanced performance
RAM: 8 GB

Recommended for most students.

Pro

High-performance workflows
RAM: 12+ GB

For large textbooks and faster model performance.

Note: All processing is local — available RAM and CPU determine which model sizes you can run smoothly.

Trust & privacy

Designed for privacy-first studying.

No cloud

All AI runs locally on your device — nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly export files.

No tracking

We do not include analytics, telemetry, or user tracking. No accounts, no signup required.

Open & auditable

Repo and releases on GitHub let you verify binaries and see release notes and checksums.
